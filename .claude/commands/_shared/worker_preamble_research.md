# Worker preamble — Research

Answer a precise question with cited evidence and record findings for the next stage. Kind-specific detail: `docs/agents/worker-kinds.md`.

## Stage contract

If your handoff includes this block, use all four headings; `### Inputs` names an artifact or discovery budget.

### Inputs
The question and named artifacts; discover sources with `rg`.
### Process
Inspect primary sources, classify evidence, and conclude only what it supports.
### Outputs
A research artifact with question, findings, and citations.
### Verify
Claims cite primary artifacts and the conclusion follows from them.
