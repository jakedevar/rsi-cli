# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in rsi.

Outside code contributions are not being merged yet. Before the first one is,
contributors will be asked to sign a contributor license agreement (CLA) so
rsi can keep being offered under both the AGPL and commercial terms.

The public repository ([rsi-cli](https://github.com/jakedevar/rsi-cli)) is a periodic export of the
private development repository, so its history arrives in sync commits.
Once outside contributions open, accepted pull requests will be ported into
the development repository by hand and credited in the next sync.

Until then, bug reports, ideas, and feedback are very welcome: open an issue
or contact the maintainer directly.

You are free to use, fork, and modify rsi under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3 (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`).
